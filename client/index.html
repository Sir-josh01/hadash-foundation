Who We Are

Driven by Compassion, Focused on Sustainable Futures

Hadash Empowerment Foundation is a newly organized movement dedicated to uplifting vulnerable children and young individuals through self-reliance programs.

Creating an Inclusive, Thriving Society

"Creating a society where everyone, regardless of their background, will be able to thrive through shared kindness and sustainable empowerment."

Capacity Building & Kindness

"To uplift individuals and communities through capacity building programs, practical charitable acts, and promoting kindness through volunteering and strategic partnerships."

Two Joined Hands & Central Heart

Our emblem represents unity, community support, and empathetic action. The two hands holding a central heart illustrate how shared kindness and togetherness protect and nurture vulnerable lives.

Our Pillars

How We Create Lasting Change

School Outreaches

Visiting primary and secondary schools to provide essential learning materials, sanitary health supplies, and mentorship drives for underage students.

  • Educational Kits & Stationery
  • Hygiene & Health Workshops
  • Inspirational Career Talks

Skill Acquisition

Replacing dependence with practical capabilities. We teach underage individuals vocational, digital, and practical life skills to foster self-sufficiency.

  • Basic Digital Literacy
  • Vocational Workshops
  • Creative & Crafts Training

Less Privileged Relief

Direct charitable interventions for vulnerable kids and families, delivering food supplies, clothing, and essential items with dignity and transparency.

  • Community Food Drives
  • Welfare Support Items
  • Emergency Relief Aid
